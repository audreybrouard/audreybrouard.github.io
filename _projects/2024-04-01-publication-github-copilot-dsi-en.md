<p class="project-publication">📰 Article published in the spring 2024 AI special issue of <a href="https://www.programmez.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Programmez!</a></p>

I had the opportunity to write about our experience introducing GitHub Copilot within Harmonie Mutuelle’s IT department.

The article looks back at our experimentation process: developer involvement, the use cases we tested, the benefits we observed, but also the limitations, security concerns, and conditions required to support adoption.

This first experiment ultimately led to the rollout of GitHub Copilot Business across the IT department.
