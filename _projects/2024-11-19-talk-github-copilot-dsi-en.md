I had the opportunity to share our experience with GitHub Copilot at FROG—the French Octocat Group, the French community of GitHub users who meet to exchange practices, use cases, and lessons learned.

An extra challenge: the talk itself was delivered in English. 🇬🇧

The goal was not to introduce GitHub Copilot or walk through its features. It was to explain what actually happens when you try to roll out this kind of tool across an entire IT department.

I shared our approach to introducing Copilot progressively, involving developers in the experiment, and—most importantly—answering a question that turned out to be far more complex than it first appeared:

How can we measure the value brought by Copilot without reducing developer productivity to a number of lines of code?
