Agents and Token Optimization is a practical guide to emerging uses of AI in software development.

Its goal is to help readers understand what happens behind an AI assistant or agent so they can frame its use more effectively, manage context, and avoid unnecessary token consumption.

The guide covers concepts that are becoming essential in agentic workflows: context windows, Prompt Engineering, Context Engineering, Loop Engineering, agents, skills, and tool use.

It also explains why the prompt represents only a small part of what a model actually receives. Files, history, instructions, tools, and intermediate results progressively enrich the context.

With agents, token consumption can grow even further as the AI analyzes, acts, verifies, and adjusts its work.

In agentic workflows, writing a good prompt is no longer enough. You also need to understand what is sent to the model, how the context evolves, and how the agent chains its actions together.

This guide offers practical reference points for adopting more effective, controlled, and token-efficient practices.
