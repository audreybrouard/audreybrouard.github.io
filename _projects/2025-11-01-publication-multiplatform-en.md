<p class="project-publication">📰 Article published in <a href="https://www.programmez.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Programmez!</a> issue 272, November–December 2025</p>

I had the pleasure of contributing an article about cross-platform development to Programmez! magazine.

I explore the main cross-platform approaches—native, hybrid, cross-platform, and PWA—along with their benefits, limitations, and the trade-offs they involve.

I also discuss React Native, Flutter, .NET MAUI, Kotlin Multiplatform, and a broader trend: the boundaries between web, mobile, and desktop are becoming increasingly blurred.

Ultimately, there is no universally ideal solution. The right choice depends on the product, the team, the constraints, and what you want to prioritize.
