<p class="project-speakers">🎤 Talk presented with Bertrand LOISEL, Tech Lead at Harmonie Mutuelle</p>

Drawing on the experience of Harmonie & Moi—Harmonie Mutuelle’s digital customer area available on the web and through iOS and Android mobile apps—we shared the architectural decisions and challenges behind a cross-platform product.

We discussed why we chose a cross-platform approach, how we handle Apple- and Android-specific behavior, validation and deployment constraints, feature flags, and application obsolescence.
